## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary B2B segments, one per flagship product, plus a broader custom-dev client base:

- **Mannaz's audience:** administrators/decision-makers at membership organizations — mutuales, sindicatos, cooperativas, clubes, colegios profesionales — who need to manage affiliates/members, visits, campaigns, and segmentation.
- **Odal's audience:** owners/operators of technical and maintenance service companies who need to manage reclamos, órdenes de trabajo, clientes, edificios, técnicos, presupuestos e inspecciones.
- **Desarrollos Personalizados' audience:** any organization with a specific digitization need not covered by Mannaz or Odal — not limited to a fixed sector.
- **Indirect/secondary:** affiliates/members of client organizations who experience the digitized service (B2C indirect, via Mannaz-using orgs).

## Product Purpose

Tecnera is a software development company based in Mar del Plata, Argentina. It builds sector-specific management products and takes on custom software development, replacing spreadsheet- and message-based manual operations with centralized digital tools. Success looks like an organization retiring its spreadsheets/disparate tools for one of Tecnera's systems and having a responsive, close relationship with the team behind it.

## Positioning

Sector-specific product fit: Mannaz and Odal are purpose-built for their vertical's actual workflow (membership management vs. technical/maintenance service operations), not a generic CRM/ERP retrofitted to a niche. A generic SaaS competitor or software agency could not truthfully claim the same fit.

## Operating Context

- Mannaz: gestión de afiliados/miembros, visitas, campañas, segmentaciones, zonas y acciones institucionales.
- Odal: reclamos y órdenes de trabajo de punta a punta, registro de clientes/edificios/técnicos, presupuestos e inspecciones; includes guided data migration from spreadsheets, per-user permissions/roles, and reporting/analytics.
- Desarrollos Personalizados: bespoke dashboards, process automation/integrations, and digitizing manual (Excel/paper) workflows for needs outside Mannaz/Odal.
- WhatsApp is the preferred contact channel in the LATAM region and is prioritized across CTAs (alongside a generic contact CTA).
- The company intends to expand beyond Mannaz and Odal into additional service offerings over time; scope of those future services is not yet defined.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products currently in production: **Mannaz** (CRM) and **Odal** (ERP). **Desarrollos Personalizados** is an active service line, not a packaged product.
- Pricing: plan tiers (Starter/Pro/Enterprise) exist as page structure, but all listed prices are "a consultar" / "a medida" — no public pricing is confirmed yet.
- Language: Spanish, rioplatense (Argentina) — tuteo/"vos", avoids excessive technical jargon.
- Terminology: "afiliados / socios / miembros" for Mannaz; "reclamos / órdenes de trabajo / técnicos" for Odal.
- Open/undecided: the shape of future service offerings beyond the two current products.

## Brand Commitments

- Company name **Tecnera**; product names **Mannaz** and **Odal** are established brands with dedicated logo/imagotipo assets (`public/products/TECNERA`, `public/products/MANNAZ`, `public/products/ODAL`).

## Product Principles

1. Sector fit over general-purpose tooling — Mannaz and Odal each solve their vertical's real workflow, not a generic CRM/ERP shell.
2. Replace manual chaos (spreadsheets, scattered messages) with one source of truth.
3. Stay accessible to small and mid-sized organizations, not only enterprise budgets.
4. Close, direct relationship with clients — small local team, WhatsApp-first contact — over anonymous-vendor distance.
5. The product line is expected to grow beyond Mannaz/Odal; custom development and future services are part of the offering, not edge cases.
